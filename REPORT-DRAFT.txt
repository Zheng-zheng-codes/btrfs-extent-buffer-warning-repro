Subject: WARNING in invalidate_and_check_btree_folios() during btrfs unmount on 7.4-rc1 (KASAN, syzkaller)

Hi Btrfs maintainers,

We observed a WARNING in `invalidate_and_check_btree_folios()` during Btrfs
filesystem unmount while running syzkaller on Linux 7.4-rc1. Because
`panic_on_warn` was enabled, the warning escalated to a kernel panic.

**Kernel information:**

- Kernel version: 7.4.0-rc1-kernsight-kasan+ #3
- Base: v7.4-rc1 (`6628e7d4c`), plus one local io_uring SQPOLL fix
  (`00043c58565e2d0d804f50c2e127f6966cd594af`); the Btrfs source is
  unmodified v7.4-rc1
- Architecture: x86_64
- Environment: QEMU Standard PC (i440FX + PIIX), KVM
- Configuration: KASAN, `CONFIG_BTRFS_DEBUG=y` (full .config in the repo)
- Fuzzing framework: syzkaller

**Warning:**

```text

------------[ cut here ]------------

```

The kernel was marked with the SOFTLOCKUP taint flag (`L`) in some of the
reports. We have not established whether an earlier soft lockup contributed
to this issue.

**Reproduction:**

- The warning was observed 19 times during concurrent syzkaller fuzzing; all
  reports share the same warning site and call chain.
- It was reproduced twice on fresh VMs by replaying a recorded program set
  (483 programs extracted from the crash-store execution logs plus the
  state-primed recipe) with
  `syz-execprog -procs=8 -threaded -repeat=0`; time to warning was about
  32 and 45 minutes of replay respectively (kernel uptime 1807 s / 2732 s at
  the warning).
- We have not yet minimized this to a single-program reproducer; automated
  minimization did not converge for this crash.
- An additional repeat run and an attempt to capture the extent buffer's
  `refs`/`flags` (running with `panic_on_warn=0`, which should let the kernel
  print the `unable to release extent buffer ... refs ... flags ...` line that
  follows the WARN) are in progress. We will follow up in this thread with
  those numbers.
